What is the First Conditional?

We use the First Conditional to talk about possible situations in the future and their results. If the condition happens, the result will happen too.

IF + PRESENT SIMPLEthe condition
,
WILL + BASE VERBthe result

Examples

Weather

If it rains, I will stay home.

School

If I study, I will pass the exam.

Practice

If she practices, she will improve.

Transport

If we don't hurry, we will miss the bus.

Present simple after IF

Never say “If it will rain”. Say “If it rains”. The future form goes in the result only.

Will + base verb

The result uses will and the base verb: I will stay, she will improve, we will miss.

Commas and negatives

Use a comma when IF comes first. Use don't or doesn't after IF, and won't in the result.
